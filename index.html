Forex position calculator

Enter your account size, risk and trade levels. You get the lot size that keeps a stop-out at the loss you chose.

–lots

Lot size is rounded down to your chosen lot step, so your real risk is never above your target. For MT4 and MT5, copy min lot, max lot and lot step from Market Watch › Symbols › Specification, because they differ by broker. Margin is an estimate; brokers set leverage per instrument. Currency conversion uses daily rates from Exchange Rate API. Metals, indices and crypto vary by broker, so check the contract size field (common defaults: 100 oz gold, 1,000 oz silver, 1 per lot for indices and crypto). A pip is 0.1 for gold, 0.01 for silver and one point for indices and crypto. Spread, slippage and broker contract sizes can change the result. This is a planning aid, not financial advice.
